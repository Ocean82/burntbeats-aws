# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-06-14*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** AI stem separation and in-browser music workstation — split, mix, export, MIDI, and vocal tools in one session.
**What it does:** Burnt Beats separates audio into stems, provides a waveform mixer and multi-stem editor, client-side export, audio-to-MIDI, vocal cleanup, and beat maker — all in the browser without a DAW install.
**Product category:** AI stem splitter / browser music production workstation
**Product type:** B2C SaaS (subscription + one-time credit packs)
**Business model:** Token-per-minute usage (1 token = 1 minute). Hybrid: free allowance + packs + subscriptions (Basic, Premium hero, Studio anchor).

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Independent producers, DJs, remixers, content creators, hobbyist musicians
**Decision-makers:** Individual creators (self-serve)
**Primary use case:** Split a track into usable stems and finish a remix or edit in the browser
**Jobs to be done:**
- Isolate vocals or instruments from a full mix
- Remix or practice with separated stems in a lightweight workstation
- Convert audio to MIDI or clean vocals without leaving the browser
**Use cases:** Remix prep, karaoke/isolation, sample extraction, vocal cleanup, beat sketching

## Personas
| Persona | Cares about | Challenge | Value we promise |
|---------|-------------|-----------|------------------|
| Bedroom producer | Speed, quality stems, export | DAW overhead for simple splits | Full workflow in browser from upload to export |
| DJ / remixer | 4-stem quality, batch, tokens | Paying for tools they use sporadically | Packs + Premium with rollover minutes |
| Casual tryer | Low friction, no subscription | Not sure if it works on their track | $0.99 single pack + 10 min welcome grant |

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Getting clean stems and a usable edit session without expensive desktop tools or opaque AI sites
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Moises/LALAL.AI: strong split but weak in-browser workstation story
- Desktop DAWs: overkill for "just split this track"
- Free tools: inconsistent quality or no export
**What it costs them:** Hours in manual editing, abandoned remixes, subscription fatigue
**Emotional tension:** "Will this actually work on my song?" — drives refunds if support is slow

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Moises, LALAL.AI, BandLab Splitter — fall short on integrated mixer/MIDI/beat workflow in one tab
**Secondary:** Desktop DAWs + plugins — fall short on speed and zero-install
**Indirect:** Manual EQ/isolation — falls short on quality and time

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Full browser workstation (not just split-and-download)
- Transparent token-per-minute pricing
- Hybrid packs + subscriptions for irregular usage
**How we do it differently:** Compute-heavy split on server; mixing/export/MIDI in client
**Why customers choose us:** One session from upload to export without leaving the tab

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Doesn't work / scam" | Route to support immediately; honest tier copy (Basic = speed 2-stem only) |
| "Too expensive" | Top-Up $5/60 min or Basic $9; Premium $15 is mid-market for 4-stem + workstation |
| "Why no 4-stem on my pack?" | Top-Up unlocks Premium capabilities while balance lasts |

**Anti-persona:** Users expecting unlimited free full-track splits with commercial API access at hobby price

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Frustration with sample-only or export-blocked trials
**Pull:** Premium workstation story — 4-stem, quality, mixer, export in one place
**Habit:** Existing DAW or competitor subscription
**Anxiety:** Quality on their specific genre; billing confusion (Basic vs Premium)
